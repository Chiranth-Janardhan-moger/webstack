WebStack is a private, offline-first visual bookmark manager for Android.

Stop keeping dozens of browser tabs open just to save articles, tools, and links. WebStack lets you save websites visually, organize them into custom tag categories, and access them instantly.

<b>Key Features:</b>

1. <b>Visual Previews</b>: Browse bookmarks as rich snapshot cards or clean, lightweight domain tiles.
2. <b>Three Layout Views</b>: Switch between large preview cards, a two-column grid, or a dense compact list.
3. <b>Instant Search</b>: Filter bookmarks in real time across titles, URLs, domains, and categories.
4. <b>Smart Tag Organization</b>: Create, rename, park, and reorder category tags to fit your workflow.
5. <b>Multi-Select & Batch Actions</b>: Long-press to select multiple bookmarks and delete them together in one tap.
6. <b>One-Tap Share Sheet</b>: Save links directly from Chrome, Firefox, Brave, or any app via the Android share menu.
7. <b>Quick Paste Detection</b>: Automatically detects links on your clipboard when adding a new bookmark.
8. <b>Standard Backup & Restore</b>: Import and export your collection anytime using standard browser HTML bookmarks.
9. <b>100% Private & Open Source</b>: No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no telemetry. All your data stays strictly on your device.
